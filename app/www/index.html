      Advanced — advisor server

      The DGX server that writes the outfit text. Reachable over Tailscale. If unreachable, the app falls back to an on-device estimate.

      What did you wear?

      Change the lines you swapped and leave the rest. The advisor learns what you reach for, and these go to the laundry pile.

      My place codes

      Your calendar writes short codes like PPK. Tell the advisor what they mean and it stops guessing — a code you have taught it is looked up, never interpreted, so it can't turn into a city on the other side of the world.

      Which calendars should I read?

      Grouped by the account each one belongs to — your e-mail address first, then its calendars underneath. Only the ones you tick are scanned for trips; birthdays, holidays and subscribed feeds are usually worth leaving off. Calendars shared with you by someone else are never read — they stay listed under their account, greyed out, and cannot be ticked.
